# Can local channels specialize as a protected spatial memory?

The previous cube recovered a hidden write trigger with a designated 32-value working bank and a designated 32-value snapshot bank. Keep both the 64 per-voxel values and the generator. Remove the assigned bank roles. Each of two physically interchangeable 32-value banks gets the same fourteen source ports (itself and the six neighbors from both banks), a command-conditioned read distribution before and after a sigmoid sensor gate, and a learned mixture at the terminal output. The two banks have separate weights, shared across bit positions, voxels and time. The bit-to-bit correspondence and two-bank grouping are supplied. A global renaming of both banks is a symmetry and cannot identify memory by itself.

**Prediction.** One bank in a neutral initialization can specialize as memory under final-output supervision. The cheaper designated-slot model is the principal alternative. First run the learnable model with a known working/snapshot initialization (biased connectivity, trainable after initialization), seed 101. If it fails to reach 90% save-dependent whole-word validation accuracy after 500 updates, stop before the three-seed test and diagnose the implementation or optimization. Otherwise train the following conditions for seeds 101, 211 and 307. Compare the ordinary 73-weight designated-slot cube with the neutral two-bank model and a matched model for which half of boards, selected independently of the task, exchange their physical banks halfway through the rollout without telling the controller. Only the latter sees this hidden exchange during training and evaluation. This control tests the stability of a physical channel address. Because the target continues to refer to pre-exchange values, the exchange can remove information about their roles. Its failure alone would not show specialization. The biased pilot is an engineering check and stays out of the three-seed comparison.

Train on fresh batches from the previous generator (batch 12, side 4, eight steps, 500 Adam updates at 0.08). Use the same paired inputs, final-bit logistic loss and fixed budget in every condition. Measure accuracy on a fixed 32-volume validation panel at steps 0, 25, 100, 250 and 500. At step 500, on validation only, identify the putative memory bank by direct sign decoding of the correct halfway snapshot on sites where the halfway working and snapshot words differ. Ties select bank 0. Keep that selection for fresh test. No architecture changes or retries based on held-out scores.

Evaluate 128 new side-six cubes for sixteen steps. Primary metric is exact word accuracy where suppressing saves changes the answer. For each board also trace the final sources back to the midpoint. For final voxels tracing specifically to a halfway snapshot whose word differed from its co-located working state, exchange that chosen bank across independent boards at the midpoint, leaving the opposite bank and all future inputs untouched. Repeat for the other bank with the same exchange. Report both scores and baselines, midpoint decoding, learning curves, learned weights, train seconds and peak device memory. Use a paired volume-and-seed bootstrap for accuracy differences.

Count evidence for an independently acquired stable memory role only if neutral reaches 90% exact accuracy and corrupting the identified bank hurts at least 25 percentage points more than corrupting its partner, with a paired interval above zero and positive selectivity for every seed. If the neutral model fails, bank assignment or its initialization remains an important prior at this budget. If it matches the designated controller, report the cost in parameters and training budget. The next experiment should target the identified bottleneck rather than sweep widths or seeds.
