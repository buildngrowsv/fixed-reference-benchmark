# Which large-model findings survive comparison with prior work?

Reviewed 19 November 2026, Chicago time. **Wu and colleagues published the sealed tunnel’s defining operation on 16 June 2026. Our best additional evidence is what happens when the early copy is replaced by an equally cheap late copy.** After adaptation on scientific Python, the late copy scored slightly better on the same 3,072 windows across six held-out projects. The wide register and spatial experiments also put useful limits on two promising small-model results. [Wu et al., §2 and §4.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.18246v1#S2) · [Our wider studies](SCALE_REVISITS.md)

## 1. The sealed forward tunnel already has an exact architectural precedent

Wu et al. truncate the active residual coordinates as the network narrows. On widening, they restore each missing coordinate from the last layer that processed it. With a single contiguous narrow segment, this is the same forward-copy operation as ours. Their language models span 200M–2B dense parameters, with a 3B mixture-of-experts extension. At 500M, their reported losses were 3.099 for carry-forward, 3.124 for zero padding, 3.150 for learned projection and 3.138 for constant width. Their choices of depth schedule, tokenization, data and comparison budgets differ from ours. Our locally developed implementation independently tests their mechanism on Python bytes. Historical priority for that mechanism belongs to prior work. The readable fixed-lane variant permits intervening blocks to read those coordinates, a different intervention. [Wu et al., 16 June 2026, §§2 and 4.4](https://arxiv.org/html/2606.18246v1#S2) · [Author implementation](https://github.com/ZhaofengWu/variable-width-transformers)

**What survives.** At 26.83M versus 31.99M weights, the compact sealed network preserved held-out CPython loss within the 0.02 bits-per-byte margin on both training orders. It retained similar prediction quality across six external Python projects in one adaptation run. This is a domain-specific efficiency result. Our comparison held updates and exposure fixed and measured parameter counts. Wu et al. compare against parameter-matched uniform networks and also report FLOPs and cache size. We have yet to demonstrate superior quality per FLOP or per second against a well-tuned uniform network at the same parameter count. [CPython scores and intervals](code_reference/sealed_study/README.md) · [External-project results](code_reference/ecosystem_study/README.md)

**What weakened.** Holding compact architecture and parameter count fixed, an early copy beat a final-working-state copy by 0.00888 and 0.00226 bits per byte on CPython, with only the first interval excluding zero. The same early slice at the defining byte caused zero double reversals on 951 and 949 initially correct identifier pairs. After scientific-Python adaptation, the working-only model scored 1.39516 bits per byte and the sealed model 1.39958. The late copy won by 0.00442 on average (95% within-project file-bootstrap interval 0.00128–0.00741). This held-out comparison counts the same 3,072 byte windows from six projects and one training order. A fixed early copy is sufficient for the compact design to work. A consistent benefit attributable to the copy has yet to emerge. Wu’s zero-fill, learned-projection and parameter-matched uniform comparisons remain important if we make a broader efficiency claim. Our late-copy control addresses a separate question, with the same weights in both arms and some duplicated exit features in the control. [CPython equal-size and swap controls](code_reference/sealed_study/README.md) · [External equal-size counts and raw data](code_reference/results/ecosystem-working)

## 2. Width exposed a training-recipe failure in register recall

Arora et al. introduced multi-query associative recall as a controlled diagnostic and tested data-dependent sparse retrieval alongside ordinary attention. Okpekpe and Orvieto examined how learning rate, width and depth affect associative recall. Our overwritten-register task distinguishes original from current values and gives one head positions derived from register identities, displaced by one event. Those details enable an intervention on a specific remembered answer. The task family, sparse memory retrieval and sensitivity to training choices have direct precedents. An exact register interpreter solves the constructed task, providing a task ceiling. [Arora et al., 8 December 2023](https://arxiv.org/abs/2312.04927) · [Okpekpe and Orvieto, 26 August 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.19029) · [Our register program and controls](register_routing/README.md)

At 512 updates the routed-minus-dense difference fell from 83.41 percentage points at 14.3M to 1.02 at 227M. The change was −82.39 points (95% paired interval −83.35 to −81.42). It supports failure to transfer this specific recipe. The wide models had roughly 0.0185 training tokens per weight, the addressed head’s share fell from one sixth to one twenty-fourth, and both wide arms finished near 3.125% chance. The original-rate dense continuation also produced nonfinite loss. Only one large counterfactual pair had both clean answers correct, making localization uninformative. A competitive control would use a stable, competent dense model, comparable routed bandwidth and a fresh evaluation set. The queued equal-rate reduction tests one explanation. It leaves head share, training exposure and seed variation unresolved. A principled width-transfer baseline is maximal-update parameterization (μP), which has its own initialization, parameterization and learning-rate rules. Our fourfold rate reduction is a simpler intervention. [Width and graft study](register_routing/large_study/README.md) · [Yang et al., 7 March 2022](https://arxiv.org/abs/2203.03466)

## 3. The wider recipient scarcely used the donated memory signal

Connecting separately trained model components through a learned interface is established. Bansal, Nakkiran and Barak tested learned stitches between frozen networks in 2021. CALM trained a small interface between frozen language models in 2024. Chen et al. demonstrated affine transfer of represented features between language models of different sizes in 2025. A trained linear alignment at our 64-value interface would be a strong baseline against inserting the donor directly into the wider host’s output columns. The informative comparison is behavioral dependence after adaptation, including the original and shuffled heads and exact query-level reversals. [Model stitching, 14 June 2021](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.07682) · [CALM, 4 January 2024](https://arxiv.org/abs/2401.02412) · [Cross-size feature transfer, 7 June 2025](https://arxiv.org/abs/2506.06609)

The 261,248-weight student previously scored 90.29% in a small hybrid. In the 227M host, aligned, shuffled and restored-native configurations scored 5.45%, 5.73% and 5.51%. Of 333 new correct answers relative to the adapted native control, four disappeared when the donor was removed. Across all queries, removal erased six answers and rescued 13. This single cross-width interface offers little evidence of donor use. The recipient was weak and used its own output projection, whereas the successful small graft inherited donor output columns. The data cannot isolate size from interface alignment. The 7B coding donor and 14B general host are a separate qualification on executable repairs. A donor lead in that screen would earn a subsequent graft test. [Query-level evidence](register_routing/large_study/graft-audit.json) · [Small hybrid](register_routing/recipient_study/README.md) · [Coding qualification](https://github.com/buildngrowsv/routed-expert-splice/blob/main/nested_surgery/LARGE_OPENER.md)

## 4. The spatial test measures role learning under one local rule

Graves et al. trained explicit read/write memory on algorithmic tasks in 2014. Kaiser and Sutskever trained locally connected gated recurrent grids on algorithms and measured length extrapolation in 2015. Etcheverry et al. reported strictly local neural cellular automata solving visual reasoning tasks in September 2026. These results establish prior art for learnable memory interfaces and repeated local computation. Our search located no direct reproduction of this exact 3D two-bank checkpointing task with paired bank swaps. That limits the comparison available here and leaves historical priority for the task open. A strong learned baseline would be a gated, channel-mixing local controller with the same neighborhood, numbers of state values, observations and training exposure. The designated snapshot controller already supplies an exact task-specific comparator. [Neural Turing Machines, 20 October 2014](https://arxiv.org/abs/1410.5401) · [Neural GPUs, 25 November 2015](https://arxiv.org/abs/1511.08228) · [Reasoning with Neural Cellular Automata, 28 September 2026](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.36126)

Increasing the neutral controller from 468 to 57,812 weights raised exact recovery from 1,071 to 1,247 of 18,544 words (+0.95 points, paired interval +0.05 to +1.84). Giving the two banks different initial roles recovered all 18,544 at both sizes. Corrupting the validation-selected bank hurt 1.06 points more than corrupting the other bank in the wider neutral model, far below the 25-point criterion. This is a reproducible endpoint for one fit. The larger rule also adds a table indexed by the sensor pattern and command. Capacity, parameterization and the frequency with which individual entries receive gradients change together. Claims about spontaneous specialization across spatial architectures would require independent training draws, sensor-pattern coverage, and comparison to the matched local controller.
