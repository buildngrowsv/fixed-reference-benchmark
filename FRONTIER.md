# Research results against prior work

Reviewed 19 November 2026. **The strongest result is a controlled transfer of a learned memory signal between small synthetic models.** A new payload-blind experiment transfers learned position scores into a separate value vocabulary. The recipient uses those scores causally, while a same-budget native router matches or beats the transplant on the longer unseen-key panel. This result supports a scoped demonstration with public audit data. [Learned-address experiment and results](register_routing/address_study/README.md)

## Register memory and the learned-address frontier

Earlier experiments provided a key-dependent list of permissible write positions. With those addresses fixed, training one receiving block improved recall from 29.08% to 90.36%. A separate initialization and training history gave 9.26% to 89.88%. Exchanging a single 64-value donor vector exchanged both answers in 2,015 of 2,017 clean correct pairs. A 261,248-weight student later replaced a 5.50M-weight donor branch and scored 90.29% against the teacher's 90.12%, with 6.98% under shuffled teacher targets. These results isolate communication, adaptation and compression under a supplied routing rule. [Recipient and compression study](register_routing/recipient_study/README.md) · [Causal swap](register_routing/prefix_study/README.md)

Cross-model components and activation interventions are established research. Bansal et al. tested model stitching in 2021. Yoon et al. transplanted selected internal modules in a February 2026 preprint. Kim et al. used a shared activation interface to pass one model's representation into another model's frozen upper layers in August 2026. Boucher's revised November 2026 working paper reports that its small synthetic graft inserted the donor's already-computed value, and that its task-vector transfer test succeeded in zero of seven open-weight-model pairs. In learned memory, pointer attention and architectures that separate address from content precede both studies. More recent associative-recall work provides benchmark and optimization controls. [Model stitching](https://arxiv.org/abs/2106.07682) · [Yoon et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.16189) · [Universal Activation Bus](https://arxiv.org/abs/2608.09521) · [Boucher working paper](https://git.spboucher.ai/wpai0001_uqo) · [Pointer Networks](https://arxiv.org/abs/1506.03134) · [Dynamic Neural Turing Machine](https://arxiv.org/abs/1607.00036) · [Revisiting associative recall](https://arxiv.org/abs/2508.19029)

The new comparison explicitly blocks the exact mechanism Boucher identified. A value-blind router emits position scores only. The host chooses from its independently encoded payloads. Cyclic shifting of the scores and source-versus-stale value replacement confirm the causal role of those scores. Results of 88.61% and 85.91% are substantial on a compound shift from 36 to 60 writes and unseen composite keys. The joint success criterion failed. Native location accuracy was near perfect and the native end-to-end result was at least as high in both fits. Thus the evidence supports transfer of **which write to read**, in an interface designed for that purpose. A competitive research claim would require a benefit over the native and symbolic components under a harder distribution, a broader family of receivers, or a setting with ordinary input streams and an emergent interface.
