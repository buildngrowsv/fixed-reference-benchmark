## Distinguishing insufficient training from a loss of the routing benefit

At 512 updates both large models scored near chance on overwritten registers, so the small difference cannot diagnose the mechanism. Continue both exact large checkpoints, including AdamW state, to 4,096 total updates. Change only training duration. Use one fresh 1,024-program panel from seed 427361 for the 512- and 4,096-update checkpoints. Report both query types at both budgets. A displaced-minus-dense advantage of at least ten points with a positive paired lower bound counts as recovery of a material route benefit. If the final displaced model answers at least half the original-value queries, repeat the direct initialization-site versus later-write swaps on 1,024 fresh pairs. If both models still fail, their capacity remains unresolved under this optimizer. This follow-up was chosen after inspecting the 512-update result.
