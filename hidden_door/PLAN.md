# Can a spatial controller infer a movable doorway from local measurements?

A periodic cubic grid carries 32 independent sign-valued channels per voxel. Movement commands identify a neighbor to read. At a region boundary, the two endpoints each carry one bit of a latch. Transport succeeds across a boundary when both bits are on. Other edges always permit the commanded move. Place one open crossing at a random coordinate on the middle dividing plane. All other boundary edges, including the wrapping seam, have at least one latch bit off. Interior readings and an additional distractor sensor are randomized. A random global complement of the region label makes the numerical label of a side uninformative. Spatial movement and the ability to mix between stay and neighbor are given. The crossing truth table and the location of the door are hidden from training.

Compare one shared neural gate that reads both endpoints with the identical-sized architecture blinded to the neighboring endpoint's latch during both training and scoring. Each receives both region labels, its local latch and two irrelevant sensor readings. Also report unrestricted movement and an exact zero-training interpreter that knows the rule. Train on final 32-bit words, without edge or intermediate labels. Use 500 Adam updates, batch 10, rate 0.015, 8 transitions on side-4 boards; each training board independently has an open or a sealed centrally placed door. Commands for the final tick at the two door endpoints request a crossing in both worlds. This gives the task a usable density of informative targets and also supplies a potential cue about *where*, but not *whether*, a crossing is open. No arm receives the action history of another cell or a door coordinate as a feature.

Evaluate paired open and sealed worlds with identical initial words, commands and nuisance sensors; modify just one endpoint latch. For a nuisance intervention, toggle only the irrelevant sensor at that same endpoint. Primary denominator is all final cells with different oracle word origins between the two worlds. Primary outcome is both 32-bit outputs exactly right on the same cell. Report each side, the untouched nuisance control, actual changes in outputs, and ordinary accuracy over all cells. Pilot on 64 validation pairs at side four and eight steps. Replicate training seeds 211 and 307 if the seed-101 full gate gets at least 80% of paired outputs correct, beats the blind gate by 20 percentage points, and more predictions change under the relevant latch edit than under the irrelevant sensor edit. Use a new final panel of 128 pairs at side eight and sixteen steps, including rotations of the barrier normal into a different axis. Training always uses axis x. All learned parameters are shared across coordinates and movement directions. Resample whole layout pairs when estimating uncertainty. Stop if transport matches the interpreter across seeds. An independently verified unsaturated task is a prerequisite for using a later graft to argue that a host needs the donor. This experiment tests learning a supplied class of local decisions and extrapolating its use, rather than global navigation or discovery of physical walls from images.
