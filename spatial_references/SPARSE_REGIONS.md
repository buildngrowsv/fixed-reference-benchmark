# Does boundary inference need the whole detector field?

Reuse the exact 96³ translated and aligned worlds, frozen weights and post-fault scoring from the inferred-boundaries comparison. Change just the boundary estimator's observation. For each axis, sample 64 transverse positions once per world from a fresh seed, and read their adjacent differences across every possible plane. The old estimator takes every transverse position. The sampled estimator sees at most 2.08% of the primary field to locate the three pairs of planes. The separate co-activation step still has the same in-region detector fields and the same one-witness budget as before. This ablation holds the transport and witness truth fixed and measures location inference, independently of end-to-end memory or wall-type changes.

Keep fixed and known-boundary controls and require exact numerical agreement with their archived runs. Compare the sampled inferred policy with the archived fully observed version on the same worlds and the same geometric bootstrap. Promote if the lower bound of sparse minus full on translated fields is at least −2 points, the sparse policy gains at least four points over fixed routing with a positive lower bound, and at least 98% of legitimate writes are accepted.
