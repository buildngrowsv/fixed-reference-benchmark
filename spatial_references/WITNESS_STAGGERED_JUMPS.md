# How quickly can the sparse observer respond to staggered boundary shifts?

The single synchronized shift study recovered 68.91% of words after the jump, above the 63.25% stale-control baseline. This follow-up uses eight fresh worlds, with one boundary jump on each axis at independently jittered rounds near rounds 5, 9 and 13. The axis order and the shifts of 28 to 68 cells are assigned by an independent world seed. The observer receives only detector samples. The stale control keeps the initial true position and the supplied-boundary control receives each updated position. The aligned companion stays fixed.

Keep the 192³ grid, frozen checkpoint, 64 lines per axis and 1,024 dedicated anchors per inferred region. The primary endpoint pools only the first round after each jump. Require at least four points of recovery gain against stale calibration with a positive lower paired bound, a lower paired bound against supplied boundaries of at least −2 points, and at least 98% legitimate writes. The bootstrap resamples complete worlds and retains all three jump rounds within each draw. Also report the latter-half outcomes, exact seam positions and tallies by world. The combined sample budget remains below 1% per observer call. [Single-jump protocol](WITNESS_BOUNDARY_JUMP.md)
