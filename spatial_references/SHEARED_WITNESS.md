# Can the sparse write observer adapt to tilted boundaries in a 3D memory grid?

The existing observer locates regional fault boundaries and picks witness directions using less than 1% of the detector field. Its prior tests shift axis-aligned walls without changing their orientation. This study applies a toroidal lattice shear so one family of boundary planes tilts relative to the physical axes. It asks whether estimating the coordinate change lets the same observer keep the right words. Coordinate transforms and equivariant networks are established precedents. This observer uses three fixed discrete coordinate hypotheses, without new training. [Sparse scale study](anchored_witness_scale_study/README.md) · [Jaderberg et al.](https://proceedings.neurips.cc/paper/2015/hash/33ceb07bf4eeb3da587e268d663aba1a-Abstract.html) · [Cohen and Welling](https://proceedings.mlr.press/v48/cohenc16.html)

Use eight disjoint 192³ worlds, frozen controller 101, 16 rounds and the previous 64 lines per axis and 1,024 witness anchors per region. The aligned companion receives no shear. For the shifted field transform canonical site (x,y,z) to physical ((x + m y) modulo 192,y,z), with a balanced randomized m in {−1,+1}. This is a permutation of cells. Movement, legitimate writes and the local detector still operate on the original physical grid. Only the geometry of false pulses is sheared.

Reuse the same stratified physical x lines when comparing slopes 0, −1 and +1. For each slope, realign their disagreement profiles and score the strongest pair of seams half a side apart. Estimate the remaining offsets and co-activation axes in that inferred coordinate frame. Map the resulting witness position back into physical space. Compare against the same observer forced to use zero shear and against an observer supplied the current shear and offsets, both with the same number of regional anchors. Search all three shear hypotheses using the same x-line values. Reading the other line families and regional anchors comes after selecting the frame. The detector-decision statistic consults at most 69,632 entries per arm. The simulation still stages dense fields.

On latter-half observations, require at least 80% exact shear recovery under nonzero shear, a two-point retention gain over zero shear with a positive paired lower bound, a lower bound of at least −1.5 points versus supplied geometry, at most a half-point paired lower-bound loss on the aligned companion, and at least 98% legitimate write acceptance. Resample complete worlds. Record counts, offset estimates and slopes to reconstruct every criterion. The study concerns a prespecified set of invertible integer shears. Arbitrary angled, curved or disconnected boundaries require a separate model. [Runner](sheared_witness.py)
