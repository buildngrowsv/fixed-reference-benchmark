# Does reusing earlier sensor evidence recover the offsets of tilted boundaries?

The sparse observer recovered the true tilt on all 64 scored rounds but located all three offsets on only 26. Retention was 68.79% with the inferred geometry versus 70.26% when supplied the exact geometry. The next experiment holds angle estimation, sensor budget, regional witness sampling, workload and weights fixed, and pools the offset score curves across earlier rounds on the same eight stationary worlds.

Compare the original same-frame observer, cumulative offset profiles and the supplied-geometry controller. Add the profile from the current observed fault tick to prior profiles for that layout. Reset history when the inferred shear changes. The phase before a fault is visible uses the same-frame observer and contributes no offset evidence. Keep all decisions prospective within the simulator, and require the reproduced same-frame and supplied controllers to match the published counts exactly. Keep the same sparse samples per event. Measure the last eight rounds and bootstrap complete paired worlds.

Require at least 90% exact tilted offset vectors, at least one point of whole-word retention gain over the same-frame observer with positive lower interval, a lower bound at least −0.75 points versus supplied geometry and no more than half a point of aligned loss at the lower bound. This test covers stationary offsets. A controller that uses accumulated evidence after real boundary jumps would need a separate reset test.
