# Open experiments and decisions

## Studio queue and current evidence

The [live queue and controls](studio_queue/README.md) show what is running, what can run next and which branches closed. The public [experiment manifest](studio_queue/queue.json) records the precommitted code and conditions.

**Fixed reference on real code.** At 16,384 updates, the 32M-parameter fixed arm scored 1.02012 versus 1.02210 held-out bits per byte. The paired package interval crossed zero. On 1,024 identifier pairs, exchanging the protected values erased 16 previously correct pairs, compared with 98 after exchanging 64 neighboring values. The independent training gate closed. Seven applications of a shared block also missed the step-matched four-block control while taking 2.23 times as long. [Study and public evidence](code_reference/README.md)

**Sealed reference.** The narrower 26,830,464-parameter middle network physically isolates the reference until the final block. After 4,096 updates it scored 1.04090 validation bits per byte versus 1.03549 for the readable 31,992,320-parameter reference. It qualified for the equal-data final test. A [precommitted independent run](code_reference/BYPASS_REPEAT.md) requires a 15% weight saving and a validation upper penalty below 0.02 bits per byte.

**Register routing.** At 512 updates the dense baseline scored 8.85% on balanced original/current queries about overwritten registers. An address-specialized head reached 93.30%, but one trained with a persistent one-event displacement reached 92.26%. Switching fixed routing after training reduced the matched model to 7.18%. The planned direct-address replication closed. A [new random-address control](register_routing/UNPREDICTABLE.md) preserves the number of available reads but removes predictable displacement. [Verified per-program counts](register_routing/results/audit.json)

**Nested Python surgery.** The smaller coding model qualified at 256/256 versus 119/256 for the unadapted host. Training around the block-27 graft yielded 335/512 versus 331 for the equally adapted native host. Turning off the donor erased 44 of 140 newly correct answers, including seven also missed by the trained native control. A four-entry suffix lookup solved all 512. A suffix-neutral opener test is queued using the very same trained graft. [Study and exact IDs](https://github.com/buildngrowsv/routed-expert-splice/tree/main/nested_surgery)

**Doorway transplant.** The learned gate with a fitted adapter solved 0/2,048 paired writes. A fresh controller and the correctly wired original donor each solved every pair. The original positive-triggered replication closed. Pubroot declined this single-seed pilot. [Audit](spatial_graft/README.md) · [Review](https://github.com/buildngrowsv/pubroot-website/issues/360)

**Third view.** The opposite camera recovered many surfaces hidden from the first camera on two synthetic depth panels, while overall accuracy fell. An independent test of routing the other view by a round-trip pixel check also lost aggregate accuracy at both camera spacings. [Protocols and evidence](https://github.com/buildngrowsv/parallax-depth-benchmark/tree/main/occlusion)

**Attention experiment completed.** Allowing the smaller coding donor to train its entire final block gained 0.13 points over its archived feed-forward-only fit on fresh confirmation families (95% paired interval −1.37 to +1.17). The full donor solved 136–142/512 compared with 172–181 for the matched-update host. The output-count lookup reached 419/512 and all three qualification gates failed. Close the feed-forward graft on this task; keep the per-repair interventions and the output-only shortcut as benchmarks for future experimental design. [Whole-block study and artifacts](https://github.com/buildngrowsv/routed-expert-splice/tree/whole-donor-block-v1/donor_block)

**Specialist qualification completed.** At block 24, 13.07M trained donor weights got 96, 100 and 102 final repairs correct out of 384; the 46.80M-weight block-28 host control got 137, 139 and 141. In the middle training order 99 new solves vanished on donor-local weight reversion, but 93 previous solves were lost by training. Swapping the learned activation change among the four requests for the same program cost 0.06 percentage points on average, with a paired 95% interval of −1.50 to +1.88. A causal removal alone can greatly overstate the portable skill. All three qualification gates failed, so there was no host graft. [Paper, intervention and full checkpoints](https://github.com/buildngrowsv/routed-expert-splice/tree/specialist-indent-v1/specialist)

**Frozen-donor screen closed.** On four-way indentation repairs with all candidates compiling, the host solved 64/256 and the donor 63/256. The host always chose four spaces, and the donor's answer was unchanged in 59/64 counterfactual quartets. No validation, test, or new graft was run after this failed discovery screen. [Protocol, released prompts and decisions](https://github.com/buildngrowsv/routed-expert-splice/tree/main/indentation)

**Wall result.** A 129-parameter local controller reading both ends recognized the hidden crossing rule on 681/681 affected held-out open/sealed pairs for every seed. Removing its far latch throughout training and scoring left the matched control with 98.76% ordinary accuracy and 0/681 paired successes. The exact graph interpreter tied the learned gate. Retire further door-size or orientation sweeps. The learned gate is available as a specialized donor if a matched, fully observed host proves meaningfully weaker. [Hidden-door study, exhaustive truth table and limits](hidden_door/README.md)

**Completed this round.** Symmetric retention peaked below the gate on fresh validation and recovered 1,071/18,544 whole words on larger cubes. The equally large role-initialized model and the cheaper designated controller got every word right. Retire the symmetric initializer at this budget. The new image-only depth study found that the two-view matcher recovered 92.51% with calibrated candidates, slightly below fixed patch matching at 92.99%. On fresh images its score fell from 92.21% to 85.94% when the calibrated shortlist was withdrawn at inference. Close the local patch-matcher branch under this renderer. [Memory pilot and intervention](persistence/README.md) · [Parallax study and open evidence](https://github.com/buildngrowsv/parallax-depth-benchmark)

## What opens the other paths

- **Expert surgery.** The off-the-shelf coding donor's semantic validation lead over a task-trained host was below the prespecified threshold and disappeared on final test. A specialized donor with a demonstrated advantage on an unsaturated task would justify another transplant. The small trained spatial write controller offers an auditable donor whose input-output rule is independently verifiable. First check whether a matched fresh controller can relearn the rule at the same budget. [Semantic qualification](https://github.com/buildngrowsv/routed-expert-splice/tree/d2062a8f88e4bb82f12c442461cd1bf601dd69dd/semantic)
- **Routing.** A confidence-based second call and a partial-verification cascade both lost to a cheaper endpoint on their respective panels. Reopen when a cheap per-example signal predicts incremental value over the strongest single-model or execution-only control. Measure prospective compute as well as solve counts.
- **Walls and geometry.** The learned local read rule obeyed a supplied wall with one doorway. The hidden-door controller subsequently learned the entire binary endpoint rule. Reopen with a change that requires information beyond those six readings and a matched fully observed baseline.
- **Learning 3D from views.** Internal 3D wiring and data-driven 3D reconstruction are separate empirical questions. Image-only supervision already recovered depth on ambiguous synthetic quartets given camera geometry, and a cheap fixed matcher did better. Reopen for an input that disambiguates currently hidden surfaces or for a new domain in which the simple geometric control fails. Do not spend a run tuning width on this renderer.
- **Scale.** Increase volume or depth only where doing so discriminates two accounts. Depth extrapolation exposed instability that a wider grid left hidden. A full-volume benchmark should report voxels, transitions, active channels, peak memory, and elapsed seconds.

## Latest public evidence

The preceding bank-role round used 500 updates for each of nine primary fits plus one calibration fit. The neutral models showed 1.25 percentage points of selective disruption, below the 25-point criterion, and an unannounced bank exchange reduced their exact-word accuracy further. GPU reloads reproduced all published counts. [Counts, curves and compute](learned_banks/results/summary.json)

The trained fixed and convex-write arms both reproduced every word on the new static-reference panel. Disabling the SAVE gate erased all correct words on save-dependent cells. When the explicit token was removed, replacing the relevant neighbor signal reduced exact recovery from 100% to 36.32%, while replacing an irrelevant signal preserved 100%. The broader research claim remains bounded by the built-in topology, state split and synthetic rule. [Study and fixed evidence](spatial_references/README.md)
